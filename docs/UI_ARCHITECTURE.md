# EigenGraph Spatial UI Architecture Contract (North Star Specification)

To maintain consistent product direction across all releases (`v0.4.8+`), EigenGraph enforces four strict UI/UX boundaries in **Spatial Mode**:

- **AI Building Design Agent & Minimalist Icon-Only Dynamic Island (`v0.8.6`)**: The resting Liquid Glass Dynamic Island capsule and 4-tab Hover Studio Deck operate strictly **100% icon-only** (zero static button words or composer placeholder sentences; full descriptions via tooltips), exposing the 6-stage `Concept -> Engineered BIM` workflow across four exclusive width-first drawers (`Site Zoning & Buildable Envelope`, `Spatial Program & 3D Stack/Pack`, `Multi-Option Massing Explorer & 1-Click Mass-to-BIM`, and `AI Building Design Agent` + archival Sheet `S-115` indexed in `S-000`).
- **`Phase 82 Field QA/QC & Rebar BBS` Drawer (`v0.8.2`)**: Width-first 2-tab drawer (`Rebar BBS & Cut-List` with pure-vector 1D stock-bar cutting pattern visualizer + `Field Waypoints & ITP/NCR` with 3D Pour-Strip/NCR overlay toggle, `Fly` camera buttons, `Clear Hold`, `Log NCR`, Sheet `S-103 .svg`, `BBS/Cut-List .csv`, and `QA/QC .ifcx`), plus `/bbs`, `/cutlist`, `/qaqc-3d`, `/waypoint`, `/shop-svg`, `/eigengate`, `/eigenflow` slash commands (`EigenGate` Linear & `EigenFlow` Non-Linear engines).
